# Six Words, One Story

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410).

## The story

> He came home. She already left.

Six words, one whole story: a man walks through his front door expecting
the life he left that morning — and the single word *already* tells you
she was gone before he even arrived. Who left whom, why, and what he does
next are all left for the reader to fill in.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes the
six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`.
